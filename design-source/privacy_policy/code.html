Information We Collect

ZettaB collects several types of information to provide and improve our services to you. We primarily collect data through our online contact forms and project inquiry tools.

  • Personal Identification: Name, email address, phone number, and company name when you submit an inquiry.
  • Project Details: Information regarding your software requirements, budget estimates, and business goals shared during consultation.
  • Technical Data: IP address, browser type, and usage patterns on our website collected via automated logs.
