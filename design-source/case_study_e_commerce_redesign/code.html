E-Commerce Platform Redesign for Global Retailer

A complete digital transformation driving global sales through performance-led design and cutting-edge cloud infrastructure.

Project Overview

Our client, a leading global retailer with operations in 14 countries, needed a modernized digital storefront to stay competitive in an increasingly mobile-first market. The goal was to unify their brand experience across regions while drastically improving site performance and checkout conversion rates.

Core Objective

Deliver a sub-2s page load time and a seamless 3-step checkout process for 50M+ monthly users.

The Challenge

speed

Performance Bottlenecks

Legacy monolithic architecture caused 6s+ load times during peak traffic, leading to high cart abandonment.

devices

Fragmented Experience

Inconsistent mobile UI across different regions resulted in a disjointed brand image and poor UX.

database

Data Silos

Inventory systems weren't synced in real-time, causing stock discrepancies and customer frustration.

security

Legacy Tech Debt

Outdated security protocols and hard-to-maintain code made rapid updates impossible.

Our Approach & Solution

We transitioned the platform from a legacy monolith to a headless, microservices-based architecture.

01

Strategic Discovery

We performed a 4-week deep dive into user behavior data and system architecture to identify the highest-impact improvements for conversion.

02

Design Systems

Created a unified Atomic Design System that ensures visual consistency across web, iOS, and Android platforms globally.

03

Technical Execution

Implemented a Next.js frontend with an AWS-hosted serverless backend to handle 100k+ concurrent users with zero downtime.

Engineered for Excellence

payments

Custom Checkout

A friction-less 3-step checkout with Apple Pay, Google Pay, and localized payment gateways integrated.

inventory_2

Real-time Inventory

Distributed WebSocket architecture ensuring stock levels are accurate down to the second across all regions.

psychology

AI Recommendations

Personalized shopping experience powered by machine learning algorithms for upselling and cross-selling.
