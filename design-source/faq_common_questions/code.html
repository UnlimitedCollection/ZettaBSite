Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our services, processes, and support. If you can't find what you're looking for, feel free to reach out.

How long does a typical web development project take?

expand_more

A typical web development project takes between 4 to 8 weeks, depending on the complexity, features required, and how quickly we receive feedback and assets from your end. We'll provide a detailed timeline during the proposal stage, broken down by specific milestones.

What is your process for managing software development timelines?

expand_more

We utilize Agile methodologies, primarily Scrum, splitting the project into 2-week sprints. This allows for regular updates, continuous testing, and flexibility to adapt to changing requirements. You will have access to a project dashboard to track progress in real-time.

Do you offer expedited delivery options?

expand_more

Yes, depending on our current team capacity, we can occasionally accommodate rush projects for an additional premium. Please mention this requirement during your initial consultation so we can evaluate feasibility.

How do you handle scope changes during a project?

expand_more

We understand that requirements can evolve. If new features are requested, we evaluate the impact on the existing timeline and budget, and issue a Change Request document. Work on the new scope begins only after your approval.

What happens if a project goes beyond the estimated timeline?

expand_more

We buffer our estimates to account for unforeseen issues. However, if delays occur due to technical hurdles on our end, we absorb the cost. If delays are caused by delayed feedback or asset delivery from the client side, it may impact the launch date and potentially incur maintenance costs.
